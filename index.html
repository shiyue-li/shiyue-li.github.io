I am a Donald J. Lewis assistant professor at the University of Michigan, mentored by David Speyer.

I work in algebraic geometry and combinatorics. More specifically, I study combinatorial structures in algebraic varieties and their moduli spaces. I am broadly interested in questions connected to number theory, algebraic topology, and algebraic K-theory.

From 2023 to 2025, I was a member at the Institute for Advanced Study, mentored by June Huh and Federico Ardila-Mantilla.

In 2023, I did my PhD in mathematics at Brown University, where I was fortunate to be advised by Melody Chan.

Prior, I spent wonderful four years at Harvey Mudd College; my undergrad thesis work was guided by Dagan Karp.

I am on the job market for academic positions, or research positions concerning AI safety and alignment (for general humanity or for mathematics), starting 2027.

Research

  1. 16

    Chow polynomials and chain polynomials of matroids are not log-concave

    with Gergely Bérczi, Basile Coron, and Chris Eur

    arXiv:2610.17135

    Abstract

    We construct explicit counterexamples from finite geometries to log-concavity of Chow polynomials, chain polynomials, and augmented Chow polynomials of matroids, disproving real-rootedness conjectures of Huh–Stevens, Ferroni–Schröter, Athanasiadis–Kalampogia-Evangelinou from the lowest possible ranks.

  2. 15

    Magic positivity of Snapper polynomials for matroids

    arXiv:2609.24917

    Abstract

    Snapper polynomials are Euler characteristics of tensor powers of line bundles on a normal complete algebraic scheme. Positivity properties of line bundles can be phrased using positivity of Snapper polynomials. We investigate their magic positivity and real-rootedness. We introduce zonotopal classes in the Grothendieck KKK-ring of vector bundles of the toric variety for any loopless matroid, and analogous classes on the Deligne–Mumford–Knudsen compactification of stable pointed curves. We prove that their Snapper polynomials are magic positive, so their coefficients are nonnegative and their h∗h^*h∗-polynomials are real-rooted. For cotangent line bundles whose first Chern classes are distinct ψ\psiψ-classes, which are not zonotopal, we nonetheless prove that their h∗h^*h∗-polynomials are real-rooted, whereas their Snapper polynomials are magic positive if and only if n⩽7n \leqslant 7n⩽7. More generally, we introduce saturated and weakly saturated KKK-classes of matroids, which furnish a sufficient and a necessary condition for magic positivity of Snapper polynomials in terms of their dragon Hall–Rado polymatroids.

  3. 14

    Matroid analogues of Gal’s conjecture

    with Basile Coron and Luis Ferroni

    arXiv:2604.04550

    Abstract

    We formulate and prove matroid analogues of Charney–Davis, Gal, and Nevo–Petersen conjectures in the setting of Chow polynomials of matroids with flag and complete building sets, which we introduce. Our proofs analyze the underlying toric varieties and show that the toric varieties undergo geometric changes that preserve γ\gammaγ-positivity, as one varies the building set to achieve complete and flag building sets. In the complete case, we construct a simplicial complex whose fff-vector is the γ\gammaγ-vector. We also obtain a new combinatorial formula for the γ\gammaγ-expansion of the Poincaré polynomial of the Deligne–Mumford–Knudsen moduli space of rational stable marked curves.

  4. 13

    Structural properties of nested set complexes

    with Basile Coron and Luis Ferroni

    arXiv:2601.05188

    Abstract

    We prove vertex decomposability and convex ear decompositions of nested set complexes. As an application, we prove that the hhh-vector is strongly flawless, and in particular, top-heavy. We specialize to the boundary complex of Deligne–Mumford–Knudsen compactification of rational stable marked curves to derive new decompositions and new formulas for its hhh-vector.

  5. 12

    Chow polynomials of rank-uniform labeled posets

    with Basile Coron and Luis Ferroni

    arXiv:2511.13819

    Abstract

    We introduce the notion of UMEL-shellable posets. They are posets admitting edge lexicographical labeling with certain uniformality and monotonicity conditions. These include uniform matroids, projective and affine geometries, Dowling geometries (including braid matroids of type A and B) and more generally rank-uniform supersolvable lattices. We prove that the Chow polynomials, the augmented Chow polynomials, and the chain polynomials associated with those posets only have real roots, thus making simultaneous progress towards conjectures by Ferroni–Schröter, Huh–Stevens, and Athanasiadis–Kalampogia-Evangelinou.

  6. 11

    Kapranov degrees

    with Joshua Brakensiek, Chris Eur, and Matt Larson

    International Mathematics Research Notices (2025) arXiv:2308.12285

    Abstract

    We introduce the notion of Kapranov degrees. These are the multidegrees of the embedding of the Deligne–Mumford–Knudsen moduli space of rational stable curves with marked points into a product of projective spaces via the complete linear series of ψ\psiψ-classes. This framework unifies intersection numbers computed by Witten, Silversmith, Castravet-Tevelev, Postnikov, Cavalieri-Gillespie-Monin, and Gillespie-Griffins-Levinson. We prove a positivity characterization and an upper bound, using matroid theory and the theory of error correcting codes.

  7. 10

    Multimatroids and rational curves with cyclic action

    with Emily Clader, Chiara Damiolini, Chris Eur, and Daoji Huang

    International Mathematics Research Notices (2024) arXiv:2311.09314

    Abstract

    We study the connection between multimatroids and moduli spaces of rational curves with cyclic action. Multimatroids are generalizations of matroids and delta-matroids introduced by Bouchet, which naturally arise in topological graph theory. This framework generalizes the previous connection between type-AAA permutohedral varieties (Losev–Manin moduli spaces) and matroids, and the connection between type-BBB permutohedral varieties (Batyrev–Blume moduli spaces) and delta-matroids. We introduce and study nef divisors and independence polytopal complexes of multimatroids. We effectively prove that the volume polynomials of these divisors are Lorentzian.

  8. 9

    Equivariant log-concavity of independence sequences of claw-free graphs

    Séminaire Lotharingien de Combinatoire 89B (2023)

    Abstract

    We prove that the graded permutation representation by independent vertex sets of any claw-free graph is strongly equivariantly log-concave. Our proof reduces the problem to the hard Lefschetz theorem on the cohomology of a product of projective lines. This gives a strengthening and a new proof of results by Hamidoune, and Chudnovsky–Seymour.

  9. 8

    KKK-rings of wonderful varieties and matroids

    with Matt Larson, Sam Payne, and Nick Proudfoot

    Advances in Mathematics 441 (2024) arXiv:2210.03169

    Abstract

    We study the KKK-rings of wonderful compactifications of hyperplane arrangement complements and of arbitrary matroids. We prove a Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch-type formula, an isomorphism between the integral KKK-rings and the Chow rings, replacing the Chern character. We introduce Euler characteristic of vector bundles of arbitrary matroids as matroid invariants. We apply these tools to Deligne–Mumford–Knudsen moduli spaces of stable rational curves with marked points.

  10. 7

    Wonderful compactifications and rational curves with cyclic action

    with Emily Clader, Chiara Damiolini, and Rohini Ramadas

    Forum of Mathematics, Sigma 11 (2023) arXiv:2208.05463

    Abstract

    We prove that the moduli space of rational curves with cyclic action, constructed in our previous work, is realizable as a wonderful compactification of the complement of a hyperplane arrangement in a product of projective spaces. By proving a general result on such wonderful compactifications, we conclude that this moduli space is Chow-equivalent to an explicit toric variety (whose fan can be understood as a tropical version of the moduli space), from which a computation of its Chow ring follows.

  11. 6

    Equivariant log-concavity of graph matchings

    Algebraic Combinatorics 6 (2023) arXiv:2202.08828

    Abstract

    We prove that the graded vector space spanned by matchings in any graph is strongly equivariantly log-concave, viewed as a graded permutation representation of the graph automorphism group. Our proof constructs equivariant injections by reducing to the hard Lefschetz theorem.

  12. 5

    Relative Bott–Samelson varieties

    La Matematica 2 (2023) arXiv:2011.04814

    Abstract

    We construct relative Bott–Samelson resolutions of singularities of Richardson varieties. We then applied it to give a resolution of singularities for the Brill-Noether variety with imposed ramification on twice-marked elliptic curves, studied by Chan–Osserman–Pflueger.
