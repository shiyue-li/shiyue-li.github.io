I am an avid amateur musician. I have played the piano since I was four and the classical guitar since I was twelve, and I love playing Bach’s music on both. Lately I have been working on the Goldberg Variations and the Chaconne, on the guitar. I also enjoy talking about music theory, composition, and ethnomusicology, and I listen widely, from jazz and world music to indie and experimental. I’d be happy to trade listening lists!

I love running on trails and feel most at home in the high mountains, where I have stood on several summits above 4,000 m in the lower 48 states. Chamonix-Mont-Blanc, in the French Alps, is especially dear to me, and my heart aches to see its glaciers retreating so quickly. I hope we act before it is too late.
