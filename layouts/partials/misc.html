I am an avid amateur musician. I have played the piano since I was four and the classical guitar since I was twelve, and I especially love playing Bach on both. Lately I have been working on the Goldberg Variations and the Chaconne from Bach’s Partita No. 2. I also enjoy thinking and chatting about music theory, composition, and ethnomusicology. I spend a lot of time exploring all sorts of music: jazz, world, indie, alternative, experimental, weird genres, etc. Feel free to exchange listening lists with me!

In my spare time, I like to trail run, and I try to run a half marathon race every year. I also love being in the high-altitude mountains and have summited several peaks above 4,000 m in the lower 48 states. A place that’s very special to me is Chamonix-Mont-Blanc in the French Alps. Sadly, its glaciers are in a devastating state, which makes my heart ache: the heat wave has brought severe melt on the Argentière and Bossons glaciers. I hope we act before it’s too late.
