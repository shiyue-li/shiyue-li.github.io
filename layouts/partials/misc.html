I am an avid amateur musician and mountaineer.

Piano

My primary instrument is the piano, which I started playing at age 4. During college, I had a blast learning from Gayle Blankenburg, as well as from a group of extremely talented peers in her studio. I’ve recently been working on:

  • Bach – Goldberg Variations, BWV 988
  • Schubert – Fantasia in F minor for four hands, D. 940
  • Beethoven – Spring Sonata for violin and piano

Classical Guitar

My secondary instrument is the classical guitar, also known as Spanish guitar, which I started learning at age 12. During college, I had fun studying guitar and baroque lute with Jack Sanders, and playing in the Pomona College Guitar Quartet from 2014 to 2016. I particularly love playing Bach's music on the guitar. I'm slowly working on:

  • Tárrega – Recuerdos de la Alhambra
  • Bach – Chaconne, Partita No. 2, BWV 1004

Other Music

I enjoy thinking and chatting about music theory, composition, and ethnomusicology. I also sang a cappella while at Yale.

Running

In my spare time, I like to run. I try to run a half marathon every year; so far, I have run the beautiful courses of the New Haven and Providence Half Marathons, and the Princeton Half Marathon three times.

Alpine Climbs

I can sometimes be found in the mountains.
